The starting point

Building should feel inspiring, not overwhelming.

Designing and building a home in Costa Rica can be one of the most meaningful projects of a lifetime. It can also become one of the most stressful.

Coordinating permits, contractors, logistics, budgets, suppliers, engineering, communication and on-site supervision —especially from another country— often creates unnecessary complexity, delays and frustration.

At Ecovilla San Mateo we created an integral ecosystem of services to help simplify that process. Our role is to support owners through every stage of the journey, with local coordination, trusted teams and a deep understanding of the community’s vision.

Who we are

Much more than construction.

This is not simply a construction service. It is a support system designed to help owners turn a vision into reality with greater clarity, efficiency and peace of mind.

We act as local allies and representatives throughout the entire process, coordinating architects, engineers, contractors, suppliers, permits and construction, while safeguarding the owner’s interests at every stage.

Why we exist

Why These Services Exist.

Over the years we saw that many owners faced similar challenges when building independently in Costa Rica:

  • Fragmented communication between suppliers
  • Unclear budgets
  • Delays and rework
  • Lack of supervision
  • Inconsistent quality
  • Logistical difficulties
  • Unnecessary stress

These services were created to reduce those points of friction and offer a more organized, transparent and supported experience. In many cases, coordinated planning and economies of scale within the community also generate significant efficiencies, both in cost and execution.

Transparency and structure

Clear rules from the start

Each service is formalized through independent contracts that clearly define scope, responsibilities, pricing structure, timelines and payment terms. Owners are completely free to contract one, two or all three services, according to their preferences and the level of support they want.

Currency and payment method

All fees are set and paid in US dollars (USD) by bank transfer, with no commissions or deductions. The fees stated constitute the final and total price of the service, with no additional charges. The client covers the transfer costs.

Confidentiality

All client information is kept strictly confidential throughout the contractual relationship.

Contract termination

By mutual agreement, for substantial breach not remedied within 15 days, or at the client’s discretion with 30 days’ notice, according to the terms of each service.

Intellectual property

The designs and drawings are the intellectual property of Ecovilla San Mateo. The client has the exclusive right to use them to build their residence, with no reproduction or reuse in other projects.

Force majeure

Neither party shall be liable for delays caused by force majeure. Timelines are automatically extended for as long as the cause persists.

Governing law

The contracts are governed by the laws of Costa Rica and subject to the competent courts of San José.
